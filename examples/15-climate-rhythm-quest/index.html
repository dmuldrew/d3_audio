📚 Scientific Datasets & Results Interpretation Guide

A multimodal empirical sonification synthesizing 147 years of official historical observations with 21st-century IPCC climate projections.

🔬 Official Datasets & Scientific Sources

  • NASA GISS GISTEMP v4 1880–2026

    Exact annual global land-ocean temperature anomalies published by the NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies. Converted from the 1951–1980 baseline to the pre-industrial 1850–1900 benchmark (+0.2°C offset) adopted in the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report.

  • NOAA Mauna Loa & Law Dome Ice Cores 1880–2026

    Historical atmospheric carbon dioxide mixing ratios (291 ppm in 1880 to 430 ppm in 2026). Combines high-resolution air samples from Antarctic Law Dome ice cores (1880–1958) with the official NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory Mauna Loa continuous in-situ record (1959–2026).

  • IPCC AR6 / CMIP6 Multi-Model Ensembles 2027–2100

    Shared Socioeconomic Pathways from the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6). Includes SSP5-8.5 (unmitigated worst-case, +4.55°C / 1,080 ppm), SSP2-4.5 (current policy pledges, +2.45°C / 603 ppm), and SSP1-2.6 (Paris net-zero, peaking at +1.52°C and resolving to +1.30°C / 414 ppm).

🎧 How to Interpret the Audiovisual Results

  • 🌀 Visual Spiral Geometry & Thresholds

    The 12 radial spokes track the calendar year (Jan–Dec). Radius represents warming: as temperatures climb, the yellow spiral unwinds outward across the concentric threshold rings (0.0°C baseline, +1.0°C modern era, +2.0°C danger zone, +3.0°C and +4.0°C catastrophic tipping points). Solid trails represent observed history; dashed trails show projections.

  • 🎵 Pitch & Lowpass Filter Brightness

    Pitch (C3 ➔ G6) tracks temperature anomaly. Low resonant pitches reflect pre-industrial stability; high pitches sound acute heat stress. Filter Cutoff (350 Hz ➔ 9,500 Hz) tracks greenhouse gas density: higher CO₂ concentrations progressively open the acoustic filter, turning muffled bass tones into sharp, sizzling brightness.

  • ⚡ Dissonance vs. Harmonic Equilibrium

    When temperatures exceed +1.5°C, FM harmonic distortion intensifies into piercing alarm frequencies. Under aggressive mitigation (SSP1-2.6 or stacked Policy Sandbox levers), the sound audibly resolves back into consonant major chords as temperatures stabilize, demonstrating acoustic equilibrium.
