🌐 How Geographic 2D Coordinates are Transformed Into Spatial Audio

Geographic Coordinate Earth Domain Acoustic Mapping Transformation Function & Spatial Rationale
Longitude ($\lambda$) -180° (W) to +180° (E) Pan: -1.0 ➔ +1.0 scalePan().domain([-180, 180]).range([-1, 1]) — Western hemisphere locations sound on the left headphone; crossing the Prime Meridian pans directly to the right headphone.
Latitude ($\phi$) -60° (S) to +70° (N) Pitch: C3 ➔ C6 scalePitch().domain([-60, 70]).range(['C3','C6']) — Southern hemisphere locations sound in low bass registers; Northern high-latitude cities chime at high treble registers.
Metro Population 5M to 38M Citizens Gain: 0.4 ➔ 1.0 scaleGain().domain([5M, 38M]).range([0.4, 1.0]) — Mega-metropolises (Tokyo, Delhi, São Paulo) produce full acoustic resonance and larger bounce shockwaves.
