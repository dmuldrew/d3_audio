🎧 How to Interpret the Audiovisual Results

  • 📈 Continuous Wave Amplitude ➔ Fluid Pitch Sweep

    Unlike discrete quantized notes, this continuous synthesizer glides smoothly along the incoming signal stream. High positive wave crests soar into bright treble pitches, while negative wave troughs dive into deep sub-bass vibrations.

  • 🔊 Rate of Change / Slope ➔ Filter Cutoff Frequency

    The first derivative (signal velocity) modulates an active lowpass filter via scaleFilter. Gradual rolling trends sound warm and muted ($1,200\text{ Hz}$), whereas sudden spikes and volatile turbulence rip open the filter cutoff to $8,000\text{--}12,000\text{ Hz}$.

  • 🖱️ Interactive Vector Dragging ➔ Spatial Stereo Panning

    Clicking and dragging inside the canvas steers the vector origin: the $X$-coordinate pans the audio between your left and right ears (scalePan), giving an immediate spatial sensation of data trajectory in real time.
