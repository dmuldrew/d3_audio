🪐 Kepler's Third Law in Action

Planetary orbital periods scale with distance ($T^2 \propto a^3$). Inner planets zip rapidly at high frequencies, while outer planets orbit at slow, resonant sub-bass registers.

• Orbital Radius ($a$) → Pitch frequency ($f \propto a^{-3/2}$).
• Planet Radius ($R_p$) → Note duration & gain.
• Transits ($0^\circ$) → Central star pulse & harmonic chime.
• Doppler Stereo → Sound pans L ↔ R as planets orbit.
