11. Ridgeline Joyplot & Topographic Sonifier

Hover ridges or sweep scan
• Horizontal Coordinate (X) → Pitch frequency & filter cutoff.
• Vertical Ridge (Y) → Base harmonic octave.
• Peak Density Height → Resonance Q & FM modulation intensity.
• Hover / Sweep → Vibrates curves like resonant physical strings.

🎧 How to Interpret the Audiovisual Results

  • 🏔️ Ridge Peak Height ➔ Acoustic Resonance (Q) & Harmonic Density

    Flat distribution tails sound like soft, diffuse background hums. As density curves crest into sharp unimodal peaks, the filter resonance $Q$ sharpens and FM modulation index rises, creating distinct, concentrated timbral focal points.

  • 📊 Horizontal Metric Value ($X$) ➔ Frequency Pitch Contour

    Moving your cursor across a ridge glides the frequency upward from left to right (scalePitch), mimicking a tactile theremin or bowed violin string along the continuous probability density function.

  • 📅 Vertical Ridge Stacking ($Y$) ➔ Octave Progression Across Series

    Lower ridges represent older chronological baselines or lower spectral tiers sounding in deep registers ($C_2 \to C_3$). Each ascending ridge shifts into higher octaves, letting the automated sweep create a cascading waterfall of harmonic textures.
