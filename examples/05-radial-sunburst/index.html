🎧 How to Interpret the Audiovisual Results

  • ⭕ Concentric Radial Depth ➔ Register & Hierarchy Level

    Inner core rings represent broad top-level taxonomic categories sounding in low fundamental octaves ($C_3$). As you move outward into child leaves, pitches climb higher into octaves $C_4$ and $C_5$, letting you discern tree depth purely by musical register.

  • 🔄 Radar Clock Sweep ➔ Cyclic Modal Time & Azimuth

    The rotating radar beam sweeps clockwise ($0^\circ \to 360^\circ$). As it intersects arc partitions, segments play in sequential succession. Clusters of small child segments produce rapid, sparkling arpeggios, while wide parent arcs sustain rhythmic space.

  • 💡 Segment Arc Area ➔ Timbral Brightness & Kinetic Pop

    Larger subtree nodes open the lowpass filter cutoff frequency (up to $8,000\text{ Hz}$), giving them prominent acoustic punch and triggering more pronounced kinetic pop blooms on the canvas.
