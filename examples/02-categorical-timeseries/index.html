🎧 How to Interpret the Audiovisual Results

  • 🥁 Activity Category ➔ Distinct Rhythmic Subdivisions (`scaleSample`)

    As the 24-hour cycle progresses, each activity category takes the lead with its own percussion voice: Sleep breathes in slow resonant sub-bass toms; Morning rings with light kalimba bells; Commuting chatters in brisk 16th-note shakers; Deep Work locks into a steady four-on-the-floor kick; Fitness erupts in driving claps and basslines.

  • 📈 Circadian Alertness Curve ➔ Pitch Elevation ($C_2 \to E_5$) (`scalePitch`)

    Pitch reflects physiological alertness throughout the day. Midnight rests in subterranean bass ($C_2 - G_2$). Morning brings ascending major triads ($G_3 \to C_4$). Midday focus soars into crisp treble arpeggios ($C_5 \to E_5$), before cascading back down to a nighttime lullaby.

  • ⚡ Biological Pace ➔ Dynamic Tempo Scaling (`scaleTempo`)

    The rhythmic tempo naturally speeds up and slows down: night hours loop at an unhurried, resting 70 BPM. Morning commutes and focus hours accelerate to 120 BPM, and evening workout sprints peak at 135 BPM.

  • 🧭 Life Domain ➔ Stereo Stage Spatialization (`scalePan`)

    Domestic home life and sleep sound on the left headphone ($-0.75$). Urban transit and commuting center in both ears ($0.0$). Workplaces, commercial hubs, and fitness centers pan to the right headphone ($+0.75$), creating an intuitive mental map of daily travel.
